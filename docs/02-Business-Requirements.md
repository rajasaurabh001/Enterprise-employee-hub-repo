# Business Requirements Document

## Project Name

Enterprise Employee Hub

## Problem Statement

Employees use multiple applications to perform daily tasks. Information is scattered across different systems, making it difficult to find resources quickly.

## Business Goal

Create a centralized employee portal that improves productivity by providing a single place to access company information, applications, and employee services.

## Stakeholders

- Employees
- HR
- Managers
- IT Team
- Leadership

## Business Objectives

- Reduce time spent searching for information.
- Improve internal communication.
- Provide easy access to company applications.
- Enhance employee experience.
- Improve productivity.

## Success Criteria

- Employees use the portal daily.
- Faster access to applications.
- Improved communication.
- High employee satisfaction.
